Terms of sale

The terms that apply when you use this website, reserve a car, test drive it or buy it from us.

Last updated 2 November 2026

Prices and listings

Prices are in Mauritian rupees and include VAT. We check every listing, but mistakes can happen. If a price or specification is wrong, we’ll tell you before you pay and you can walk away. A car is only sold once we’ve signed a sale agreement together.

Monthly payments

Monthly figures on this website are estimates at the rate shown. The lender sets the final rate and terms after approving your application. Finance is subject to status.

Reserving a car

You can reserve a car with a refundable deposit of Rs 25,000. We hold it for seven days. If you decide not to buy, or your finance isn’t approved, we refund the deposit in full.

Test drives

Bring a valid driving licence. One of our team comes with you. You’re responsible for fines incurred during the drive.

Warranty

Every car comes with the warranty shown on its page, starting on the day you collect it. It covers the engine, gearbox and major electrical parts. It does not cover wear items such as tyres, brake pads and wiper blades, or damage from accidents or misuse. Your rights under the Consumer Protection Act are not affected.

Selling your car to us

Online valuations are a rough guide. Our written offer follows an inspection and is valid for seven days. We pay once the car and its documents are handed over, and we handle the NLTA transfer.

Using this website

Don’t misuse the forms or try to break the site. Car photos are licensed from Unsplash. Questions about these terms: hello@v8motors.mu.
