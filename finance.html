Questions people ask

Who provides the finance?

We work with the main local banks and leasing companies. We send your application to the ones most likely to approve it at a good rate, and you choose which offer to take.

How much deposit do I need?

Most lenders ask for at least 10% of the car’s price. A bigger deposit lowers your monthly payment and the total interest.

Can I use my current car as the deposit?

Yes. We value your car and that value comes off the price. If you still owe money on it, we settle that with your bank.

What do I need to apply?

Your national ID card, proof of address, your last three payslips and three months of bank statements. Self-employed buyers also need recent accounts.

How long does approval take?

Usually two to four working days once we have all your documents. We keep the car for you while you wait.

Can I pay it off early?

Yes. Most lenders allow early settlement. Some charge a small fee, which we tell you about before you sign.
